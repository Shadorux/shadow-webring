FAQ

What kind of site can join?

Shadow the Hedgehog shrines, fan sites, archives, blogs, tools, graphics sites, and other independent projects are welcome. A larger site can also join if it has a substantial Shadow-focused section.

Does my site need to be big or popular?

No. Tiny personal sites are explicitly welcome. The point of a webring is discovery, not follower counts.

Do I need a GitHub account?

No. Email your submission to support@shadorux.dev and the registry can be handled for you.

What does the widget do?

It adds Previous, Random, and Next links so visitors can travel between member sites in the ring.

Can I style the widget to match my site?

Yes. The important part is keeping working webring navigation visible to visitors. Your site can still look completely like your own.

Is this official?

No. Shadow Webring is an unofficial fan project by Shadorux and is not affiliated with or endorsed by SEGA.
